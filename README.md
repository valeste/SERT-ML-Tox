__Background and Rationale__ 

- Concerns using experimentally determined human receptors and transporters for current molecular docking workflow 

- No experimentally determined ERa or SERT structures for a Puget sound target species (Chinook salmon) have been identified in RCSB Protein Data Bank (PDB). 

- Species-specific differences in receptor and transporter structures in fish may influence ecological relevance of chem prioritization. 

- Homology modeling offers an approach to generate predicted Chinook SERT and ER structures using experimentally determined hSERT and hER templates available on RCSB PDB.
